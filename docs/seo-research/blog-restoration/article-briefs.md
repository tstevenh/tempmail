### Research notes

DataForSEO returned metrics for all 25 configured keywords. The clearest measured opportunities were `temporary email services` (40,500 US monthly searches), `burner email address` (27,100), `throwaway email address` (14,800), the three “best” variants (1,900 each), and `create temporary email address` (1,000). All reported competition values were LOW, but this is paid-search competition and must not be treated as organic ranking difficulty.

The DataForSEO SERP task returned nine organic domains for `best temp mail` and zero organic results for the other four primary queries. Those empty responses are an API-evidence limitation, not proof that Google has no results. Live search was therefore used to inspect five visible results for each query. Search results vary by time, location, personalization, and index freshness. Lengths below are estimates from visible body content or stated reading time, not audited word counts.
